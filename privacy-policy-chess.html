Legal — Chess Offline

Privacy Policy

Chess Offline is an iOS game built by Robert Libsansky. It does not collect, store or transmit any personal data — this page explains exactly what that means.

Robert Libsansky built the Chess Offline app as a game for iOS. This service is provided as is, and this page is used to inform you about my policies regarding the collection, use and disclosure of information if you decide to use it.

The short version: the app collects no data at all, so there is nothing for me to use, share or lose.

I collect no data

The app never asks for your name, email address, phone number or any other personal information. No account and no sign-in is required.

It does not connect to the internet

Chess Offline contains no networking code. The chess engine is the app's own and runs on your device, the pieces are drawn from vector paths in code, the sounds are generated at launch and the puzzles ship inside the app, so everything works entirely offline — the home-screen widget included.

No analytics, no advertising

The app contains no ad networks, no analytics frameworks and no third-party libraries that transmit anything anywhere. It links only Apple's own frameworks.

No in-app purchases

The app contains no in-app purchases and no subscriptions. Nothing in it is for sale, and no feature — the game review included — is limited by a quota or held back for payment.

Where your progress is stored

Your settings, the game you have in progress, your finished games and their reviews, your statistics and achievements, your puzzle rating and history, the positions collected from your own mistakes, your opening-trainer progress and which chapters of the guide you have read are stored solely on your device, in the app's own files and the system UserDefaults store. This data never leaves the device. It is included in your regular device backup (iCloud or computer) if you have one enabled, in which case Apple's own policies apply. Deleting the app removes this data from your device.

Files you choose to share

You can export games as a PGN file through the system share sheet, and import PGN files you pick in the system file picker. The app only reads a file you have chosen, and only sends a file where you send it; it does neither on its own.

The clipboard

On the Analysis Board, Copy FEN puts the current position on the clipboard, and the Paste PGN or FEN screen lets you paste in a game or a position. The app reads the clipboard only when you tap its Paste button, through the system's own paste control, and only to read the game or position from it. It never reads the clipboard on its own.

Crash reports from Apple

If you have chosen in your device's privacy settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide me with crash reports and aggregated usage statistics for the app. They come from Apple, not from the app itself, contain nothing that identifies you, and are covered by Apple's privacy policy. I use them only to fix bugs. Whether they are shared is your choice, made in your device's settings.

Device access

The app does not use the camera, microphone, location services, contacts, health data or any other sensitive permission. It only uses haptic feedback and audio playback.

Children

The game is rated 4+ and is suitable for any age. Because the app collects no data, no data about anyone of any age is created.

Changes to this privacy policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. You are advised to review this page periodically.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact me at rob@hotmail.cz. If you write to me, I see your email address and your message, and I use them only to reply to you.
