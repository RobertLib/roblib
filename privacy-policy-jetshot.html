Legal — Jetshot

Privacy Policy

Jetshot is an iOS game built by Robert Libšanský. It collects no personal data — this page explains exactly what that means.

Robert Libšanský built the Jetshot app as a game for iOS. This service is provided as is, and this page is used to inform you about my policies regarding the collection, use and disclosure of information if you decide to use it.

The short version: Jetshot collects no personal data. The only thing that ever leaves your device is your own game progress, and it goes to your own iCloud account.

We collect no data

The app never asks for your name, email address, phone number or any other personal information. No account and no sign-in is required, and nothing about you or your play is sent to me or to anyone else.

No analytics, no advertising, no tracking

The app contains no ad networks, no analytics frameworks and no third-party SDKs of any kind. It links only Apple's own frameworks. There is no advertising identifier and no tracking of any sort.

No in-app purchases

The app contains no in-app purchases and no subscriptions.

Where your progress is stored

The following is stored on your device using the system UserDefaults store:

  • Completed levels
  • Level scores
  • Star ratings
  • Weapon loadouts
  • Endless-run records

iCloud synchronization

The same five values are mirrored to Apple's iCloud Key-Value Storage so that your progress follows you between your own devices and survives reinstalling the app. That data lives in your personal iCloud account, is readable only on devices signed in with your Apple ID, and I have no access to it — there is no server of mine involved at any point. If you would rather keep your progress on one device only, turn iCloud Drive off for Jetshot in Settings → your name → iCloud. The game works normally without it.

Everything else runs offline

All content — levels, graphics, music and sound — ships inside the app. Apart from the iCloud sync above, the app makes no network requests.

Device access

The app does not use the camera, microphone, location services, contacts, health data or any other sensitive permission. It only uses haptic feedback and audio playback.

Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data, no data about children is created either.

Deleting your data

RESET on the level-select screen erases your progress from the device and from iCloud. Deleting the app removes the local copy; the iCloud key-value data is removed with the app's iCloud data in Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage.

Changes to this privacy policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. You are advised to review this page periodically.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact me at libsansky@iquest.cz.
