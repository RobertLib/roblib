Legal — Wordwander

Privacy Policy

Wordwander is an iOS game built by Robert Libsansky. It does not collect, store or transmit any personal data — this page explains exactly what that means.

Robert Libsansky built the Wordwander app as a game for iOS. This service is provided as is, and this page is used to inform you about my policies regarding the collection, use and disclosure of information if you decide to use it.

The short version: the app collects no data at all, so there is nothing for me to use, share or lose.

I collect no data

The game never asks for your name, email address, phone number or any other personal information. No account and no sign-in is required.

It does not connect to the internet

Wordwander contains no networking code. Every puzzle and every word list, in English and in Czech, is inside the app; the daily puzzle is picked from the date on your device; and the sounds are generated on the device. Everything works entirely offline.

No analytics, no advertising

The app contains no ad networks, no analytics frameworks and no third-party libraries that transmit anything anywhere. It links only Apple's own frameworks.

No in-app purchases

The app contains no in-app purchases and no subscriptions. The coins you spend on hints are earned by playing and cannot be bought.

Where your progress is stored

Your levels, coins, streaks, achievements, the words in your lexicon and your settings are stored solely on your device, in one file in the app's own storage. None of this ever leaves the device. Your progress is included in your regular device backup (iCloud or computer) if you have one enabled, in which case Apple's own policies apply. Deleting the app removes this data from your device.

Daily reminder

If you turn on the daily reminder in Settings, the app asks for permission to show notifications and then schedules them on your device. They are local notifications: no server is involved and nothing is sent anywhere. The reminder is off until you turn it on.

Word definitions

The lexicon shows definitions from the dictionaries installed on your device, through iOS's own dictionary viewer. The app itself sends nothing anywhere when you look a word up.

Crash reports from Apple

If you have chosen in your device's privacy settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide me with crash reports and aggregated usage statistics for the app. They come from Apple, not from the app itself, contain nothing that identifies you, and are covered by Apple's privacy policy. I use them only to fix bugs. Whether they are shared is your choice, made in your device's settings.

Device access

The app does not use the camera, microphone, location services, contacts, photos, health data or any other sensitive permission. It only uses haptic feedback, audio playback and, if you turn on the reminder, notifications.

Children

The game is rated 4+ and is suitable for any age. Because the app collects no data, no data about anyone of any age is created.

Changes to this privacy policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. You are advised to review this page periodically.

Contact and your rights

Robert Libsansky of Prague, Czech Republic, publishes the app and is the controller of the personal data described on this page. If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact me at rob@hotmail.cz.

If you write to me, I see your email address and your message. I use them only to reply to you, which is my legitimate interest under Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR. They stay with my email provider, Microsoft (Outlook.com), only as long as the conversation needs, and I delete them sooner if you ask. You have the right to access, correct or erase that data, to restrict its processing and to object to it, and to lodge a complaint with the Czech data protection authority, the Office for Personal Data Protection (uoou.gov.cz).
